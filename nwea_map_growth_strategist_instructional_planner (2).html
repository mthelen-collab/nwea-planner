    About NWEA RIT & Instructional Minutes

    What is a RIT score? The Rasch Unit (RIT) scale is an equal-interval vertical scale that measures student achievement regardless of grade level.

    Proficiency Level: Corresponds approximately to the 50th percentile (national grade-level norm) or state proficiency cut scores.

    Advanced Level: Corresponds to the 80th-90th percentile, indicating readiness for accelerated learning, honors, or gifted/talented coursework.

    Instructional Minutes Calibration: Derived from National Center on Intensive Intervention (NCII) and NWEA growth study norms. Closing a 5–10 RIT gap typically requires 90–150 additional minutes of targeted intervention weekly above core instruction over a 12-week semester.
